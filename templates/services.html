COMMON QUESTIONS

Before We
Start Building.

Can Nexora handle both design and development?+
Yes. The experience is planned, designed and engineered as one system, so visual quality and technical quality do not fight each other.
Can you build a website with advanced 3D interaction?+
Yes. 3D experiences can include interactive objects, product visualization, WebGL scenes, motion-driven storytelling and virtual spaces, depending on the project.
Do you also build dashboards, portals and business software?+
Yes. Nexora can build authenticated applications, admin dashboards, booking systems, internal tools, databases, APIs and custom workflows.
Will the final website work properly on mobile?+
Responsive behavior is designed from the beginning. Interactions are adjusted for touch devices rather than simply shrinking the desktop experience.
Can you maintain the product after launch?+
Yes. Ongoing maintenance can cover technical updates, performance, content changes, analytics, feature improvements and future expansion.
