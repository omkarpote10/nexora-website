Privacy Notice

This is a starter privacy notice for the Nexora website. Before commercial launch, have it reviewed for your exact business, tools, analytics, hosting, geography and legal requirements.

Information we collect

When someone submits a project enquiry, Nexora may collect information such as name, company, email address, phone number, project goals, requested features, budget range, timeline and other details voluntarily provided in the enquiry form.

How we use information

Information submitted through the website is used to review enquiries, communicate about potential projects, prepare estimates or proposals, manage follow-up and improve the service experience.

Storage and access

Project enquiries may be stored in Nexora's internal lead-management system. Access should be limited to authorized people responsible for handling enquiries and client relationships.

Analytics and cookies

This starter build does not assume a specific analytics or advertising setup. If analytics, advertising pixels, session recording or other tracking technologies are added later, this notice should be updated and any required consent mechanism should also be added.

Third-party services

Nexora may use hosting, email, database, communication or other infrastructure providers. Those services may process data as part of operating the website and client communication.

Retention

Lead information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for business communication, record keeping and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

Visitors may contact Nexora to ask about information previously submitted through the website, subject to applicable law and verification requirements.
